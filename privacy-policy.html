Privacy Policy

Welcome to Al-Bader (also known as Adhkar App). We are dedicated to providing a rich, trustworthy, and spiritually uplifting mobile experience while upholding the highest standards of data privacy. This Privacy Policy details how our mobile application handles information, device permissions, media streaming, community features, and in-app purchases.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information (Authentication)

When you choose to sign in to Al-Bader (for example, to post advertisements or manage community listings), we authenticate your account using Google Sign-In on Android, Apple Sign-In on iOS, and Firebase Authentication. We collect:

  • Your basic profile name and email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • A unique authentication user identifier (UID)

Note: Core spiritual features (Daily Adhkar, Quran reading, Dhikr Counter, Prayer Times, Radio) are fully accessible without creating an account.

B. User-Generated Content & Community Submissions

If you actively submit content to our community modules, we collect the information you provide:

  • Job Vacancies: Job title, description, company/organization name, location, and contact phone number.
  • Matrimonial Proposals: Bride/groom profile details, age, educational background, district, family information, and guardian/contact phone numbers.
  • Buy & Sell Ads: Item title, category, price, product photos, description, and contact phone number.

C. Automatically Processed Information

To ensure app stability and optimal performance, we collect standard device metrics (such as device model, OS version, and network status). We do not collect or track Advertising Identifiers (IDFA / GAID).

D. Alarms, Reminders & Local Preferences

When you set up custom alarms (such as Tahajjud, Suhur, or personalized reminders) or configure prayer notification preferences, all associated settings—including alarm title, trigger times, recurrence days, and chosen audio sounds (Adhan or beep)—are stored strictly locally on your device (using local storage). This data is never transmitted to our servers, backed up to third-party clouds, or shared with external parties.

2. Multimedia Streaming & External Content Delivery

Al-Bader provides access to external Islamic multimedia content, including Islamic Status Videos, Audio Recitations (Adhkar, Surahs), and Live Islamic Radio Streams:

  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Media files are streamed securely from external cloud servers (such as Cloudflare R2 and broadcast streaming servers like Zeno.fm). When you stream or download media, standard technical communication occurs between your device and the hosting server (such as your IP address and standard HTTP request headers).
  • Local Caching & Offline Storage: Audio files and status videos may be cached locally on your device storage to allow smooth playback and offline viewing. You can clear this cache at any time through the app settings or by clearing app storage.
  • No Content Profiling: We do not track, profile, or sell your listening or viewing history to any third-party advertisers.
  • Content Ownership & Integrity: All religious multimedia content, audio recordings, and status videos are curated strictly for non-commercial spiritual, educational, and inspirational purposes with full respect for copyright and content owners.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Al-Bader requests specific device permissions solely to provide features you explicitly activate:

  • Location (GPS - When In Use): Used solely while using the app (Foreground / "When In Use") to calculate local prayer timetables, indicate the Qibla direction, and show nearby masjids and Ziyarath sites. We do not request, require, or run background ("Always") location services. When calculating prayer times for GPS-detected coordinates, your approximate latitude and longitude are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to the AlAdhan API to fetch astronomical prayer times; AlAdhan processes these coordinates in real-time without storing user identity or profiling. When you search for nearby masjids, coordinates are queried against OpenStreetMap Overpass or Photon services. Your location is never tracked in the background, logged permanently, or sold to third parties.
  • Camera: Used exclusively when you open the built-in QR Scanner to scan Al-Bader QR codes. The camera stream is analyzed on-device in real time and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Photos / Photo Library: Used only when you select and attach an image to a Buy & Sell listing or community posting. We never access your photo library without your explicit selection.
  • Notifications & Alarms (Alerts & Sounds): Used to deliver timely prayer time alerts (Adhan / Baank), user-defined Islamic custom alarms (Tahajjud, Suhur), and daily morning/evening dhikr reminders. On iOS and Android, notifications require your explicit permission to present alerts, display banners, and play audio. Audio files (such as the Adhan or alarm tone) are bundled locally within the application and played directly on your device. You can customize audio tones, adjust notification timings, or disable notifications entirely at any time in the app settings or system settings.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Al-Bader offers optional premium digital features and support subscriptions. All financial transactions are processed securely through:

  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit) on iOS devices
  • Google Play Billing on Android devices
  • RevenueCat for managing digital purchase entitlements

We do not collect, process, or store your credit/debit card details, banking credentials, or billing address. All payment processing complies with Apple's and Google's strict security standards.

5. Advertising & Tracking

No Third-Party Advertising: Al-Bader does not display third-party banner or video advertisements and does not use advertising tracking networks. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure to operate the application:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure user login and storing community posts.
  • Apple Sign-In: For optional account authentication on iOS.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass and Photon: For nearby masjid search results when you request them.
  • RevenueCat: For verifying digital receipt entitlements.
  • Cloudflare R2 & Workers: For high-performance media delivery and video streaming.
  • AlAdhan API (Islamic Network): For astronomical prayer time calculations. Receives latitude and longitude coordinates over HTTPS to return accurate prayer schedules. No user accounts, personal identities, or tracking tokens are sent.

7. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

We believe in giving you complete control over your personal data:

  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated submissions directly inside the application by navigating to Profile / Account Settings → Delete Account.
  • Instant Data Removal: Deleting your account immediately removes your profile, active listings, and associated data from our active Firebase database.
  • Manual Request: You may also request complete removal of any listing or data by emailing us at salahudheen9745@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Al-Bader provides religious, spiritual, and educational content suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately.
